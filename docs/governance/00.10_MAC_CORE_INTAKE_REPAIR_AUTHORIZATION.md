The LLM MUST NOT be treated as the cryptographic authority for `source_digest`.

The repair must provide a deterministic trusted boundary for required provenance metadata. A valid implementation may derive the digest from the exact real user-turn text or a canonical host-provided representation, but it must make the derivation deterministic, testable and traceable to a `source_ref` that identifies the actual user-turn/source instance.

If the available OpenCode 1.18.3 tool execution context does not expose enough native user-turn identity/content to establish the required provenance without trusting LLM-supplied identifiers, STOP and return to 00.10 with exact host-contract evidence. Do not invent message IDs, hashes, timestamps or provenance.

`observed_at` and other provenance fields must come from a deterministic trusted boundary or an explicitly source-backed value; they must not be semantic guesses by the model.
